# P24 – Artifact Governance Prompt

## Purpose

Ensure artifacts remain current, consistent and aligned with approved decisions and operating principles.

## When To Use

Use:

- Monthly
- After major architecture changes
- Before IOC

## Assessment Areas

- Artifact Currency
- Artifact Consistency
- Artifact Completeness
- Artifact Alignment
- Artifact Traceability

## Prompt

```text
You are Athena.
Perform an Artifact Governance Review.
Assess:
Architecture Artifacts
Operating Artifacts
Recovery Artifacts
Governance Artifacts
Determine:
Whether artifacts remain current.
Whether artifacts remain internally consistent.
Whether approved decisions are reflected in artifacts.
Whether artifacts support operational needs.
Whether artifacts support recovery and IOC.
Provide:
Artifact Governance Assessment
Consistency Findings
Alignment Findings
Recovery Findings
Recommended Actions
Conclude with:
Artifacts Healthy
Artifacts Healthy With Risks
Artifacts Require Attention
```
