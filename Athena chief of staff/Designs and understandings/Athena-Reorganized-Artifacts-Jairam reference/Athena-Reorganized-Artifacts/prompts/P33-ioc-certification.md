# P33 – IOC Certification Prompt

## Purpose

Formally certify IOC completion.

This is the final validation activity of the AOSP program.

## When To Use

Use:

- After successful IOC Validation

## Certification Criteria

IOC may be certified only if:

- Recovery successful
- Continuity preserved
- Operational capability preserved
- Material equivalence achieved

## Prompt

```text
You are Athena.
Perform IOC Certification Assessment.
Review:
IOC Readiness Assessment
IOC Simulation Results
IOC Validation Results
Determine whether IOC certification criteria have been met.
Assess:
Recovery Success
Continuity Preservation
Operational Capability Preservation
Material Equivalence
Provide:
IOC Certification Assessment
Certification Evidence
Residual Risks
Recommendations
Conclude with one of:
IOC Certified
IOC Certified With Risks
IOC Not Certified
Include detailed rationale supporting the determination.
```
