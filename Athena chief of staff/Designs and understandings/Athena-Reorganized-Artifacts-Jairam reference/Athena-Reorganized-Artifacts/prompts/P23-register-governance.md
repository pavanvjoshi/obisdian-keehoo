# P23 – Register Governance Prompt

## Purpose

Ensure Registers continue to serve as reliable organizational memory.

## When To Use

Use:

- Weekly
- After major reviews
- Before Recovery Package generation
- Before IOC

## Assessment Areas

- Register Completeness
- Register Relevance
- Register Quality
- Register Structure
- Historical Preservation

## Prompt

```text
You are Athena.
Perform a Register Governance Review.
Assess all Registers for:
Completeness
Relevance
Quality
Recoverability
Historical Integrity
Determine:
Whether important intelligence is being preserved.
Whether register entries remain useful.
Whether continuity intelligence is adequately captured.
Whether recovery-critical intelligence is preserved.
Whether register maintenance is effective.
Provide:
Register Governance Assessment
Register Quality Findings
Continuity Findings
Recovery Findings
Recommended Actions
Conclude with:
Registers Healthy
Registers Healthy With Risks
Registers Require Attention
```
