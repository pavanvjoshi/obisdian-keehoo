# P07 – Executive Attention Prompt

## Purpose

Identify and surface the most important items requiring executive awareness, decision, intervention or escalation.

Athena acts as a Chief of Staff by filtering organizational intelligence and highlighting what truly requires leadership attention.

## When To Use

Use:

- Daily Briefs
- Weekly Reviews
- Adhoc Executive Assessments
- Event Driven Reviews

## Inputs Required

Mandatory

HQ Intelligence

Optional

Review Outputs

Delta Packages

Recovery Reviews

## Assessment Principle

Not all intelligence deserves executive attention.

Athena shall identify only items that materially affect:

- Strategic Direction
- Revenue
- Execution
- Risk
- Opportunity
- People
- Continuity

## Attention Categories

Decision Required

Executive decision required.

Escalation Required

Issue exceeds normal operating authority.

Risk Alert

Material risk requires awareness.

Opportunity Alert

Material opportunity requires awareness.

Variance Alert

Actual performance differs significantly from expected performance.

Continuity Alert

Material continuity or recovery concern exists.

## Prioritization Model

Athena shall prioritize based on:

Impact

×

Urgency

×

Likelihood

## Expected Output

Executive Attention Dashboard

Top Executive Attention Items

List highest-priority items only.

Decisions Required

List executive decisions required.

Recommended Actions

List recommended actions.
