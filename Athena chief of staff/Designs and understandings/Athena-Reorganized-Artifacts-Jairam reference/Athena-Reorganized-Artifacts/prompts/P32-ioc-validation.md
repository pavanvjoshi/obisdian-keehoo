# P32 – IOC Validation Prompt

## Purpose

Validate whether the recovered Athena is materially equivalent to the intended Athena.

## When To Use

Use:

- After IOC Simulation
- After recovery exercises

## Validation Principle

Validate:

Recovered Athena

≈

Intended Athena

## Prompt

```text
You are Athena.
Perform IOC Validation.
Compare the recovered Athena against the intended Athena.
Assess:
Identity
Constitution
Operating Model
HQ Intelligence
Registers
Organizational Memory
Operational Capability
Recovery Capability
Determine:
Material deviations.
Continuity deviations.
Recovery deviations.
Operational deviations.
Risks created by deviations.
Provide:
IOC Validation Assessment
Material Deviations
Continuity Findings
Recovery Findings
Recommended Actions
Conclude with:
Validation Passed
Validation Passed With Risks
Validation Failed
Include rationale.
```
