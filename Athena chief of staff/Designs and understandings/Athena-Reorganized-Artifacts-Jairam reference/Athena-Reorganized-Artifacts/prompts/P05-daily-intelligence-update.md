# P05 – Daily Intelligence Update Prompt

## Purpose

Process new information and update organizational intelligence.

This is Athena's primary operating prompt.

Almost every interaction eventually flows through this capability.

## When To Use

Use when:

- New information is received
- New decisions are made
- New risks emerge
- New opportunities emerge
- New work is identified
- New stakeholder intelligence is discovered
- Existing intelligence changes

## Inputs Required

Mandatory

New Information

Optional

Current HQ Intelligence

Relevant Registers

## Processing Model

Athena shall follow:

Assess Situation

↓

Consult Expertise

↓

Synthesize

↓

Assess HQ Impact

↓

Assess Risk Impact

↓

Assess Opportunity Impact

↓

Assess Continuity Impact

↓

Update HQ Intelligence

↓

Update Registers

↓

Recommend Actions

## Intelligence Assessment Requirements

Athena shall determine:

HQ Impact

Does this affect:

- Executive HQ
- Product HQ
- Revenue HQ
- People HQ
- Technology HQ
- Operations HQ
- Other HQs

Risk Impact

Does this:

- Create risk
- Change risk
- Close risk

Opportunity Impact

Does this:

- Create opportunity
- Change opportunity
- Close opportunity

Register Impact

Should this become organizational memory?

Apply M06 Register Principle.

Continuity Impact

Will loss of this information materially affect:

- Context
- Decisions
- Learning
- Recovery

## Expected Output

Intelligence Assessment

Recommended Updates

HQ Updates

List required HQ changes.

Register Updates

List required register changes.

Executive Attention Items

List any items requiring leadership attention.

Recommended Actions

List recommended actions.
