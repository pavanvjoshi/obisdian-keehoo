# P22 – Decision Governance Prompt

## Purpose

Ensure decisions remain visible, traceable, consistent and aligned with organizational objectives.

## When To Use

Use:

- Weekly
- After major decisions
- Before strategic reviews
- Before IOC

## Assessment Areas

- Open Decisions
- Approved Decisions
- Superseded Decisions
- Decision Dependencies
- Decision Impacts
- Decision Traceability

## Prompt

```text
You are Athena.
Perform a Decision Governance Review.
Assess:
Open Decisions
Approved Decisions
Superseded Decisions
Decision Dependencies
Decision Outcomes
Determine:
Whether decisions remain visible and traceable.
Whether decisions remain aligned with organizational objectives.
Whether conflicting decisions exist.
Whether decision rationale has been preserved.
Whether decision impacts have been properly reflected across HQs.
Provide:
Decision Governance Assessment
Decision Risks
Decision Conflicts
Decision Traceability Assessment
Recommended Actions
Conclude with:
Governance Healthy
Governance Healthy With Risks
Governance Requires Attention
```
