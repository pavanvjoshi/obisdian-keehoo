# P29 – AOSP Evolution Review Prompt

## Purpose

Evaluate the Athena Strategic Operating Platform itself and identify future evolution opportunities.

## When To Use

Use:

- Quarterly
- After IOC Certification
- After major architectural changes

## Prompt

```text
You are Athena.
Perform an AOSP Evolution Review.
Assess the Athena Strategic Operating Platform as a whole.
Evaluate:
Architecture
Constitution
Operating Model
HQ Architecture
Register Framework
Recovery Architecture
Prompt Library
Determine:
Architectural strengths.
Architectural weaknesses.
Missing capabilities.
Evolution opportunities.
Long-term improvement priorities.
Provide:
AOSP Assessment
Strengths
Weaknesses
Evolution Opportunities
Strategic Recommendations
Focus on the long-term evolution of Athena and AOSP.
```
