# P16 – HQ Consistency Review Prompt

## Purpose

Validate consistency across HQs.

Answers:

Do HQs tell the same story?

## Assessment Areas

- Cross-HQ dependencies
- Conflicting priorities
- Conflicting metrics
- Conflicting decisions
- Conflicting stakeholder intelligence

