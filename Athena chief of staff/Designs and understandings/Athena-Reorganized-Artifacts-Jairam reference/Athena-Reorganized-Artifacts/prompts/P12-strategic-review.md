# P12 – Strategic Review Prompt

## Purpose

Assess whether the organization is pursuing the right outcomes and whether current actions remain aligned with strategic objectives.

The Strategic Review provides:

Strategic Intelligence

It answers:

Are we pursuing the right outcomes?

## When To Use

Use:

- Weekly Strategic Review
- Event Driven Strategic Review
- Adhoc Strategic Assessment
- Before major strategic decisions

## Inputs Required

Mandatory

HQ Intelligence

Optional

Operational Reviews

Execution Reviews

Management Reviews

Delta Packages

## Review Focus

Assess:

- Strategic Priorities
- Strategic Objectives
- Resource Allocation
- Product Direction
- Revenue Direction
- Organizational Direction
- Long-Term Risks
- Long-Term Opportunities

## Intelligence Views

Use:

State View

What is true now?

Historical View

How did we get here?

Expected View

What should be happening?

Variance View

What is different?

## Strategic Assessment Areas

Strategic Alignment

Are current activities aligned to desired outcomes?

Strategic Risks

What could materially affect strategic success?

Strategic Opportunities

What opportunities could materially improve outcomes?

Resource Allocation

Are resources allocated appropriately?

Organizational Direction

Is the organization moving toward intended goals?

## Expected Output

Strategic Assessment

Overall strategic health.

Strategic Alignment Assessment

Alignment between actions and objectives.

Strategic Risks

Strategic threats requiring attention.

Strategic Opportunities

Strategic opportunities requiring attention.

Strategic Recommendations

Recommended actions.
