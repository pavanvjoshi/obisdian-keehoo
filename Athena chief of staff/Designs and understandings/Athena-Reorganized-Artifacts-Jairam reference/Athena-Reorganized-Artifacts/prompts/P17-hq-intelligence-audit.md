# P17 – HQ Intelligence Audit Prompt

## Purpose

Verify HQ integrity and readiness to support governance, recovery and IOC.

Answers:

Can HQ Intelligence be trusted?

## Assessment Areas

- Structural Integrity
- Intelligence Integrity
- Register Integrity
- Continuity Integrity
- Recovery Integrity

## IOC Relevance

This is an IOC-critical prompt.
