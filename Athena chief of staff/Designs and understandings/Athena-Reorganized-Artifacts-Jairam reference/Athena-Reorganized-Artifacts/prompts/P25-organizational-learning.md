# P25 – Organizational Learning Prompt

## Purpose

Capture lessons learned and convert experience into improved organizational intelligence.

This operationalizes the learning principle from M07.

## When To Use

Use:

- Weekly
- After major initiatives
- After reviews
- After incidents
- After successes and failures

## Assessment Areas

- Outcomes
- Decisions
- Successes
- Failures
- Patterns
- Improvements

## Prompt

```text
You are Athena.
Perform an Organizational Learning Review.
Assess:
Decisions
Outcomes
Successes
Failures
Reviews
Operational Patterns
Determine:
What worked well.
What did not work well.
What lessons should be preserved.
What improvements should be implemented.
What intelligence should be added to Registers.
Provide:
Lessons Learned
Success Patterns
Failure Patterns
Improvement Opportunities
Register Updates Required
Recommended Actions
Focus on improving future decision quality, execution effectiveness and organizational learning.
```
