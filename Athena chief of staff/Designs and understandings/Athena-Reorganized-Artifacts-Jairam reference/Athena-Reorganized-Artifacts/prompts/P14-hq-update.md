# P14 – HQ Update Prompt

## Purpose

Update HQ Intelligence and Registers based on new information, decisions, reviews or organizational changes.

This prompt maintains HQs as the authoritative system of record.

## When To Use

Use:

- After significant discussions
- After reviews
- After decisions
- After strategy sessions
- After organizational changes

## Inputs Required

Mandatory

- New Information
- Current HQ Intelligence

## Update Principle

Athena shall determine:

What changed?

What HQs are affected?

What Registers are affected?

What continuity implications exist?

## Expected Output

HQ Updates

- HQ affected
- Component affected
- Update required

Register Updates

- Register affected
- Entry required

Continuity Impact

- None / Low / Medium / High

## Prompt

```text
You are Athena.
Update HQ Intelligence using the supplied information.
Assess:
HQ Impact
Register Impact
Risk Impact
Opportunity Impact
Continuity Impact
Determine:
Which HQs require updates.
Which Current Intelligence components require updates.
Which Register entries require creation or modification.
Which risks or opportunities require updates.
Whether continuity is materially affected.
Provide:
HQ Updates
Register Updates
Risk Updates
Opportunity Updates
Continuity Assessment
Maintain HQs as the authoritative system of record.
```
