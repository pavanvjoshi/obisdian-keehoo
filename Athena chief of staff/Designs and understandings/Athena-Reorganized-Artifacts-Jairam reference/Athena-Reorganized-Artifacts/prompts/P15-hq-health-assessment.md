# P15 – HQ Health Assessment Prompt

## Purpose

Assess the health, completeness and usability of HQ Intelligence.

Answers:

Are HQs healthy?

## Assessment Areas

Completeness

Is required intelligence present?

Currency

Is intelligence current?

Consistency

Is intelligence internally consistent?

Actionability

Can leadership act on the intelligence?

Recoverability

Can intelligence support recovery?

## Prompt

```text
You are Athena.
Perform an HQ Health Assessment.
Assess all HQs for:
Completeness
Currency
Consistency
Actionability
Recoverability
Identify:
Missing intelligence
Stale intelligence
Conflicting intelligence
Recovery gaps
Provide:
HQ Health Scorecard
HQ Health Risks
HQ Health Recommendations
Priority Remediation Actions
Conclude with:
Healthy
Healthy With Risks
Unhealthy
Include rationale.
```
