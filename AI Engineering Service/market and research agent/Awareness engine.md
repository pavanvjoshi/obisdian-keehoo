## The Main Idea

Most sales systems work like this:

> "Find as many contacts as possible and send messages."

That approach mostly creates spam.

An awareness engine works differently.

Instead of asking:

- "Who can we contact?"

It asks:

- "Who is entering a situation where our product suddenly becomes useful?"

That is the entire difference.

---

# Think About It Like This

Imagine you sell umbrellas.

A normal sales system:
- collects names of random people
- sends umbrella advertisements to everyone

Most people ignore it.

But an awareness engine:
- watches the weather
- detects rain
- finds people outside without umbrellas
- contacts them at the right moment

Now the message makes sense.

The timing matters.

The context matters.

The awareness matters.

---

# What The Awareness Engine Actually Does

The engine continuously tries to answer 4 questions:

```text
1. Who are they?
2. What problem are they likely facing?
3. Why would they care right now?
4. Which of our products can help them?
```



### Lead generation:

lead generation is the process of identifying the potential customer who shows interest in a product or service. 

 **Static** - this is fixed, pre-existing list of contacts information often purchased or compiled from outdated sources. 
 * it is like a company purchasing outdated emails list of 'marketing managers' from a third party and sending a generic one size fit all email blast to everyone on the list, without personalization and segmentation. 
**Database** - using structured , searchable database to find and target prospects. 
* a sales person uses a paid service like **Zoominfo**, **apollo.io** etc. to search for and export a list of up-to date contact information for "marketing managers at tech companies in new york"
**Contact-centric** - Focusing on deep personalisation and relationship building for each individual. 
* a marketer attends a tech event, speaks with an attendee about thier challenges and later sends a personalised email referencing their conversation and offering a relevant solution.
