	2. It routes information into **Knowledge Management**.
3. Conversation Layer
	1. Athena Conversation Engine or chief of staff
	2. Sits between KNowledge base and personas
4. Personas Involved
	1. Management / company board
	2. PMO (Product management office)
	3. PO (product Owner)
5. System behavior
	1. It is a **role-aware enterprise assistant** that:
		* ingests organizational data,
		- keeps knowledge updated,
		- supports conversations,
		- and delivers different views based on user role.
	2. **Natural language interaction** through a conversational assistant ("Athena").
	3. **Agent orchestration**, where a central decision engine routes requests to specialized domain agents.
	4. **Role-specific AI assistants** for executives, PMOs, product teams, and content management.
	5. **Collaboration workflows** supporting one-time tasks, incremental ("delta") updates, and usage analytics.
	6.  **Enterprise reporting and governance**, enabling automated summaries, compliance checks, and strategic insights.
