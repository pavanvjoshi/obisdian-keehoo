# Business Type — Software / SaaS

> Any startup where the product is software delivered as a service — app, platform, tool, API.

← [[journey-index|Journey Index]]

---

## Examples
EdTech app, HR software, logistics SaaS, productivity tool, B2B API, consumer mobile app.

## The Metrics That Matter

| Metric | Definition | When it matters |
|---|---|---|
| MRR | Monthly recurring revenue | Stages 4–7 |
| Churn rate | % of customers who cancel each month | Stage 5 onwards |
| CAC | Cost to acquire one paying customer | Stage 3 planning |
| LTV | Average revenue per customer over their lifetime | Stage 5 onwards |
| NRR | Net Revenue Retention — are customers paying more over time? | Stage 7 |
| DAU / WAU | Daily / weekly active users | Stage 4 onwards |
| Trial → paid conversion | % of free users who become paying | Stage 4 |
