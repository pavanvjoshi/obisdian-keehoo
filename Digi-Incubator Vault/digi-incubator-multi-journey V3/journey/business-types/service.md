# Business Type — Service Business

> Any startup where the product is delivered by people — skills, time, expertise, care.

← [[journey-index|Journey Index]]

---

## Examples
Physiotherapy clinic, design agency, tutoring centre, legal services, cleaning service, accounting firm, logistics company, consulting practice.

## The Metrics That Matter

| Metric | Definition | When it matters |
|---|---|---|
| Monthly revenue | Total revenue (not "recurring" — no subscription) | Stage 4 onwards |
| Utilisation rate | % of available capacity that is booked and delivering | Stage 5 |
| Revenue per staff member | Monthly revenue divided by headcount | Stage 7 |
| Average ticket size | Revenue per engagement / project / session | Stage 4 |
| Repeat client rate | % of clients who return | Stage 5 |
| Referral rate | % of new clients who came from existing client recommendation | Stage 5 |
| Lead time | Time from inquiry to first appointment / project start | Stage 4 |

## Agent Behaviour by Stage

| Stage | Agent | What it asks |
|---|---|---|
| 3 | [[../agents/gtm-agent\|GTM]] | How do first clients find you? Referrals, local SEO, partnerships? |
| 3 | [[../agents/financial-projections-agent\|Financial Projections]] | Capacity × utilisation × ticket size. Staff cost as primary variable |
| 7 | [[../agents/unit-economics-monitor\|Unit Economics]] | Utilisation rate. At what % are you profitable? |
| 7 | [[../agents/hiring-plan-agent\|Hiring Plan]] | Second delivery person first — revenue cannot grow without capacity |

## The Key Scaling Question

> *At what utilisation rate do all fixed costs get covered — and what team size gets you there sustainably?*
