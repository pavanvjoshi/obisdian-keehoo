# Business Type — Deep Tech / R&D

> Any startup where the product requires significant scientific or engineering research before it can be commercialised.

← [[journey-index|Journey Index]]

---

## Examples
Biotech startup, climate tech company, semiconductor design, defence hardware, agri-science, medical device, research spin-off from a university lab.

## The Metrics That Matter

| Metric | Definition | When it matters |
|---|---|---|
| Runway (months) | How long the money lasts at current burn | Always |
| Monthly burn rate | How much you spend per month | Always |
| TRL level | Technology Readiness Level (1–9) | Stages 3–6 |
| Grant funding secured | Non-dilutive capital from govt / research bodies | Stages 3–6 |
| IP filings / patents | Intellectual property protection | Stage 4 onwards |
| Pilot / PoC partners | Organisations co-developing or testing with you | Stage 4 |
| Regulatory milestone status | Where you are in any required approval process | Stage 5 onwards |
| Time to first commercial contract | Projected date of first paying customer | Stage 6 planning |

## TRL Reference

| Level | What it means |
|---|---|
| 1–3 | Basic research and concept — no prototype |
| 4–5 | Validated in laboratory / simulated environment |
| 6–7 | Prototype demonstrated in relevant environment |
| 8–9 | System complete and qualified — ready for deployment |

## The Key Grant Landscape (India)

| Body | Programme | For whom |
|---|---|---|
| DST | NIDHI, TIDE 2.0 | Early-stage technology startups |
| BIRAC | BIG, SBIRI | Biotech and life sciences |
| DRDO | Technology Development Fund | Defence and aerospace |
| MEITY | Software Technology Parks | IT and electronics |
| DPIIT | Startup India Seed Fund | DPIIT-registered startups across sectors |

## The Key Question

> *Are you on track to hit the milestones your current grant requires — before the money runs out — and do those milestones set you up for the next grant or first commercial contract?*
