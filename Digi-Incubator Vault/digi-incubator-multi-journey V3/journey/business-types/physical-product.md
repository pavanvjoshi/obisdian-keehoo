# Business Type — Physical Product

> Any startup where the product is something you manufacture, source, or curate — and ship or sell in physical form.

← [[journey-index|Journey Index]]

---

## Examples
Food brand, FMCG product, handicraft / artisan goods, hardware device, agricultural produce brand, D2C consumer product.

## The Metrics That Matter

| Metric | Definition | When it matters |
|---|---|---|
| Units sold / month | Volume of product moving | Stage 4 onwards |
| Gross margin per unit | (Selling price − COGS) / selling price | Stage 3 planning |
| COGS | Cost of goods sold — raw materials, packaging, manufacturing | Stage 3 |
| Distributor margin | % you give up to reach retail shelves | Stage 3 |
| Repeat purchase rate | % of buyers who buy again | Stage 5 |
| Inventory turnover | How many times you sell through inventory per month | Stage 7 |
| D2C vs distributor revenue split | How much comes direct vs through channel | Stage 5 |

## Agent Behaviour by Stage

| Stage | Agent | What it asks |
|---|---|---|
| 3 | [[../agents/gtm-agent\|GTM]] | Which distributors, what margin, how to get first retail placement? |

## The Key Scaling Question

> *At what monthly unit volume does gross margin cover all fixed costs — team, rent, logistics?*
