## What It Assembles

The platform has been storing every agent run, every milestone, every financial projection since Stage 1. The data room agent organises this into the sections a Series A investor expects.

| Section | Source |
|---|---|
| Business overview & market opportunity | [[lean-canvas-agent\|Lean Canvas]] + [[market-research-agent\|Market Research]] |
| Traction evidence | All `startup_milestones` with timestamps |
| Financial model — actuals vs projections | [[financial-projections-agent\|Financial Projections]] runs compared |
| Team and hiring plan | [[hiring-plan-agent\|Hiring Plan Agent]] output |
| Use of funds (current round) | Burn Tracker history |
| Ask | Founder writes this |

## What It Cannot Do

Write your narrative. Explain your vision. Tell your personal story.

Those are yours. The data room agent handles the evidence — you handle the meaning.
