## The First Question

Before running, the agent reads `business_model_type` from the `startups` table and branches its entire question set.

## By Business Type

### SaaS / Software
Focuses on digital channels, trial structure, onboarding flow, and conversion rate from free to paid. Asks: which communities, which SEO terms, what in-app trigger converts a free user to paid?

### Physical product
Focuses on physical channel strategy — wholesale, D2C, modern trade, kirana network. Asks: which distributors will stock you, at what margin, and what do you need to do to get a reorder?

### Service business
Focuses on referral networks, local discovery, and partnerships with complementary services. Asks: how do your first 10 clients find you, and what does the referral flywheel look like?

### Marketplace
Produces **two separate GTM plans** — one for supply side, one for demand side. Asks: which side is harder to get? (Usually supply.) Start there. Demand follows supply density.

### Deep tech / R&D
Focuses on securing the first pilot partner, not acquiring customers. Maps the grant landscape and matches the startup to applicable schemes (DST, BIRAC, NIDHI, DRDO). Asks: who will co-develop with you before you have a commercial product?
