## The Rule

> Hire the person who removes your biggest constraint — not the person who sounds most impressive.

## By Business Type

### SaaS
**First hires:** Engineer 2 + Customer Success person.
You need more product and you need to keep the customers you have. Don't hire a salesperson before you have a repeatable sales motion — they'll spend 6 months figuring out what you haven't figured out yet.

### Physical product
**First hires:** Supply chain coordinator + D2C marketing person.
Your two biggest risks are running out of stock at the wrong moment and not building a direct customer relationship. Both need a dedicated human.

### Service
**First hire:** A second service delivery person.
You cannot grow revenue without more capacity. Hire the person who delivers the service before you hire anyone who sells it.

### Marketplace
**First hires:** One person for supply onboarding + one for demand acquisition. These must be two separate people. Both sides need someone whose only job is growing them.

### Deep tech
**First hires:** A second researcher or engineer + a part-time regulatory/compliance consultant.
Move faster on the science. Have someone who can read the compliance landscape without it becoming your full-time job.
