
### Idea

* q1: An AI legal document assistant that helps small businesses understand contracts by translating legal language into plain English and highlighting risky clauses
* q2 - Small business owners sign contracts without fully understanding the legal implications.
* q3 - SaaS
* q4 - semi technical
* q5 - Actively building
* q6 - Freelancers and small agency owners
* q7 - Bangalore, hyderbad, Pune

---
### Lean canvas

1. **Customer Segments:** Primary: Freelancers, small agency owners, startups, and small business founders who regularly sign client, vendor, employment, or partnership contracts. Target orgs: Digital agencies, IT service companies, consulting firms, and early-stage startups without dedicated legal teams. Secondary: Independent consultants, HR agencies, procurement teams, and startup incubators needing quick contract understanding and risk analysis.
2. **Problems:** Small business owners often sign contracts without fully understanding legal language and hidden risks. Hiring lawyers for every agreement is expensive and time-consuming. Important clauses related to liability, termination, payment terms, and exclusivity are frequently overlooked, leading to financial or legal issues.
3. **Unique Value Proposition (UVP)**: An AI-powered legal assistant that converts complex legal contracts into plain English, highlights risky clauses, summarizes obligations, and helps small businesses confidently review agreements without depending entirely on lawyers.
4. **Solution:** A SaaS platform where users can upload contracts and instantly receive simplified explanations, clause summaries, risk alerts, negotiation suggestions, and key obligation tracking. The platform supports PDFs, DOCX files, and scanned agreements with AI-based legal analysis.
5. **Channels:** LinkedIn marketing, startup communities, freelancer forums, SEO blogs about contract review, YouTube educational content, Product Hunt launches, partnerships with incubators and coworking spaces, and targeted digital ads in Bangalore, Hyderabad, and Pune.
6. **Revenue Streams:** Monthly and yearly SaaS subscriptions, freemium plans with limited contract reviews, premium team plans for agencies and startups, API integrations for legal-tech platforms, and white-label solutions for legal consultants or firms.
7. **Cost Structure:** AI infrastructure and API costs, cloud hosting, OCR/document processing systems, software development, legal expert consultation, cybersecurity and compliance, marketing campaigns, and customer support operations.
8. **Key Metrics:** Number of contracts analyzed, monthly active users, customer retention rate, free-to-paid conversion rate, average contract review time, risk detection accuracy, user engagement, and recurring monthly revenue.
9. **Unfair Advantage:** Specialized focus on small businesses and freelancers, AI trained specifically for contract simplification and risk detection, affordable alternative to expensive legal consultations, localized understanding of Indian business contracts, and growing proprietary legal-risk datasets improving model accuracy over time.
